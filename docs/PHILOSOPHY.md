## Founding premise

> Kali is the condition of an age — greed, deception, conflict, craving without limit, the decline of dharma. Kalki is what restoration looks like once those conditions end. Their purposes stand opposed. But opposition is not enmity — nothing here requires imagining two conscious beings at war.

Restoration isn't a battle StarVault wins. It's a threshold the current data economy already crossed.

## I. The conditions — कलियुग (Kali Yuga)

In a data economy, Kali Yuga doesn't arrive as myth. It arrives as product design: three conditions, each one shipped as a feature.

| Term | IAST | Gloss | As it appears |
|---|---|---|---|
| लोभ | lobha | Greed | Data brokers price and resell behavior no one agreed to sell. The transaction happens; the consent doesn't. |
| काम | kāma | Craving | Engagement systems are tuned to exploit attention, not serve it — craving manufactured and sold back as relevance. |
| मोह | moha | Delusion | Consent becomes theater: a wall of legalese standing in for understanding, agreed to by nobody who read it. |

Compounded, these three are अधर्म — **adharma**. Not sin. Just the default setting of a system nobody was asked to consent to.

## II. The restoration — कल्कि (Kalki)

The protocol is what सत्य (truth) looks like once it's owed back.

| Term | IAST | Gloss | Protocol mechanism |
|---|---|---|---|
| सत्य | satya | Truth | Append-only audit — every access leaves a record nobody can quietly edit afterward. |
| शौर्य | śaurya | Courage | Deny-by-default consent — nothing is granted until someone actively says yes, and revocation is immediate, not negotiated. |
| करुणा | karuṇā | Compassion | The vault stays free by design. Trust isn't a tier you pay to unlock. |

None of this needs villains. It needs a system that stops defaulting to adharma — which is the only fight this doctrine actually proposes.

## Positioning
