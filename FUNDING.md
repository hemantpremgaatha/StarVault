# Support StarVault

StarVault is the network layer for human consent in the AI economy — an open
protocol (SVP) plus reference apps for identity, consent, and revocable data
access. It's built in public, and funding goes directly toward development
time, infrastructure (hosting, HIBP/security APIs), and protocol documentation.
